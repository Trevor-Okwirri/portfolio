# 🌍 Working Remotely as a Developer in Kenya

Working remotely as a developer in Kenya is both exciting and challenging. The internet has opened doors to global opportunities, allowing Kenyan developers to collaborate with clients and teams across India, Europe, and the US — often from a small workspace or a local coffee shop.  

But landing those remote roles isn’t always easy. Many global companies still overlook African talent, and some platforms have regional restrictions or bias toward certain time zones. Payment hurdles, verification delays, and unreliable internet connections can also slow things down.  

Still, the landscape is improving fast. Strong portfolios, consistent delivery, and clear communication make a real difference. Developers who learn to document well, manage async communication, and build trust find that location becomes less of a barrier.  

Remote work has redefined what’s possible from Kenya — the challenge now is not access to tools, but access to opportunity. And that’s slowly changing with every successful project shipped from this side of the world.
