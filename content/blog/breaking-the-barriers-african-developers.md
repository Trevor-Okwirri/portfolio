## 🌍 The Shifting World of Work

The global job market has changed forever. Remote work has gone from *trend* to *infrastructure.*  
Yet, for many African developers, that world still feels out of reach.

::u-alert{title="The Paradox of Access" icon="lucide:scale" color="neutral" variant="soft"}
We have the internet, but not always access.  
We have the skills, but not the visibility.  
We are remote, but not considered <em>remote enough.</em>
::

Despite the challenges, a quiet movement is happening.  
From Nairobi to Lagos, Kigali to Accra, developers are learning how to make the system work *for* them, not *against* them.

---

## 🧭 Step 1: Build an Irresistible Digital Presence

Visibility is currency.  
If you’re not visible online, in global terms, **you don’t exist.**

::u-alert{title="Quick Win" icon="lucide:lightbulb" color="primary" variant="soft"}
Start small. Pick one platform — LinkedIn, GitHub, or a portfolio site — and commit to <em>showing your work</em> weekly.
::

### Portfolio
Your portfolio is your handshake with the world.  
It should be clean, fast, and personal — not just a list of projects but a **story of growth**.

- ✅ Show projects with measurable results  
- ✅ Add “how I built this” case studies  
- ✅ Include contact links (Calendly, email, etc.)  

### GitHub
Your GitHub speaks louder than your CV.
- Contribute to **open-source projects** (even documentation!)  
- Use **GitHub Actions** or **readme stats** to show consistency  
- Add thoughtful READMEs that demonstrate communication skills  

### LinkedIn
Recruiters *live* on LinkedIn. Optimize your profile like it’s your product.  
Use banners, write posts, and share your journey — even small wins attract opportunities.

## 💬 Step 2: Build Bridges, Not Just Résumés

Networking is often misunderstood. It’s not about cold DMs — it’s about **being seen where ideas live.**

Places to connect:
- 🗣️ **Twitter/X Spaces** — join African tech talks & threads  
- 💻 **Discord / Slack** — Laravel, React, or Vue communities  
- ✍🏾 **Hashnode & Dev.to** — share what you’re learning  
- 🌐 **Remote Africa**, **Remotive**, **Turing**, **We Work Remotely** — platforms that support African remote talent

::u-alert{title="“Visibility builds trust”" icon="lucide:message-circle" color="warning" variant="soft"}
People don’t hire what they can’t see.  
Engage genuinely — comment, share, and help others. That’s how credibility compounds.
::

## 🗣️ Step 4: Communicate Like a Global Pro

The biggest skill gap isn’t technical — it’s **communication.**  
Global teams thrive on clear, proactive communication.

### What Global Employers Value
- Consistency > Genius  
- Clarity > Complexity  
- Team empathy > Individual speed

**Practical Habits:**
- Use short **Loom** videos when submitting deliverables  
- Summarize meetings in shared docs  
- Write async-friendly updates (“Here’s what I did, what’s next, what I need”)  

::u-alert{title="Communication Hack" icon="lucide:brain" color="secondary" variant="soft"}
Practice explaining your work as if to a non-developer. The clearer you are, the more leadership you project.
::

---

## 💰 Step 5: Negotiate With Confidence

If a company values your work globally, your pay should reflect that.

> “We pay based on local cost of living” is not a justification — it’s an outdated mindset.

**How to Respond:**
- Reference global pay data from Levels.fyi or Glassdoor  
- Suggest a **trial month** to prove your worth  
- Emphasize <em>value</em>, not <em>location</em>  

::u-alert{title="Don’t Apologize for Your Rates" icon="lucide:ban" color="error" variant="soft"}
Global work deserves global pay. Period.
::
